## Students & School Life

Recent reports on Chicago Public Schools highlight significant trends in student enrollment and school utilization across the district. A notable development indicates that 158 schools within Chicago currently have more empty desks than enrolled students.

This finding points to a substantial number of schools operating below capacity, suggesting ongoing challenges related to declining student populations in various neighborhoods. Such data is critical for district leaders and stakeholders as they consider future planning, resource allocation, and facility management strategies for Chicago's public education system.

## Early Childhood Education

The Chicago Public Schools (CPS) board is actively considering the expansion of early childhood education programs across the district. Recent discussions have focused on exploring options for implementing full-day preschool services specifically for 3-year-olds.

Board members have indicated a desire to study the feasibility and scope of broadening these early childhood services. This development suggests an ongoing process of deliberation regarding potential policy changes or increased investment in early learning opportunities for younger children within the CPS system.

## Board of Education & Governance

The upcoming historic Chicago School Board election is a central theme in recent news, with significant media attention focused on informing voters. Candidate forums, such as those hosted by WTTW and reported by the Chicago Tribune, have provided platforms for candidates for School Board President to discuss key issues. WBEZ has also published voter guides and highlighted key education endorsements, underscoring efforts to engage the public and clarify candidate positions.

Policy debates among candidates have centered on critical issues including school budgets and the contentious topic of school closures, where candidates have expressed divergent views. A notable emerging issue is the use of Artificial Intelligence in schools, with some candidates signing a pledge to ban its use. Separately, the current Board of Education is considering plans to study and potentially expand early childhood programs and services. In a related financial development, S&P recently revised the Chicago Board of Education's outlook to negative.
