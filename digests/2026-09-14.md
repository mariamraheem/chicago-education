## Board of Education & Governance

The Chicago Board of Education is actively considering an expansion of early childhood education and preschool services, with members discussing a potential study to explore these programs. In other operational matters, the Board withdrew a $5.5 million proposal intended to support the ChiArts conservatory model. These discussions occur as S&P Global Ratings recently revised its outlook on the Chicago Board of Education's bonds to negative, indicating increased scrutiny of the district's financial position. Separately, Chicago Public Schools implemented its extreme heat policy during the past week.

The upcoming school board elections are a significant focus, with several candidates publicly committing to a pledge to ban the use of Artificial Intelligence in schools, signaling an emerging policy debate. Campaign financing is also a topic of discussion, with reports detailing the Chicago Teachers Union's expenditures on various school board candidates. Furthermore, candidates vying for the Board of Education president position have raised questions regarding a specific "do-not-hire" appeal, highlighting a focus on personnel and accountability issues as the election cycle progresses.
